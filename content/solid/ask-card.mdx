## Usage

```tsx
import { AskCard } from "@/components/ui/ask-card"
```

```tsx
<AskCard
  question="Raise the timeout and rerun, or leave them out?"
  options={["Raise and rerun", "Leave out"]}
  onAnswer={send}
/>
```

## After the answer

Once the conversation has moved on, leave `onAnswer` out. The choices are then no longer offered, and the card stays as a record of what was asked. Give `chosen` the answer and it is a record of what was said too: that choice keeps its pane, the others fade to words.

```tsx
<AskCard question={question} options={options} onAnswer={answered ? undefined : send} chosen={answered} />
```

The choices are not disabled once answered, only inert: a disabled button drops the keyboard to the page on the very press that answers.
